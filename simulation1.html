About This Simulation

This basic evolution simulation demonstrates how simple organisms evolve through natural selection. Each entity has a chromosome that determines its traits including color, size, speed, vision range, longevity, and reproduction rate.

Key Mechanisms

Mutation

Random changes in offspring traits create genetic diversity.

Selection Pressure

Limited food resources create competition for survival.

Reproduction

Successful organisms pass traits to their offspring.

Educational Insights

This simulation illustrates several fundamental concepts in evolutionary biology:

Fitness refers to an organism's ability to survive and reproduce in its environment. In this simulation, fitness is determined by how well an organism's traits match the environmental conditions.

Notice how certain traits become more common over time as they prove advantageous for survival and reproduction.

Adaptation is the process by which populations become better suited to their environment over generations.

Try changing the food distribution pattern and observe how the population adapts with different traits becoming dominant.

Speciation occurs when populations diverge to form distinct species. While this simulation doesn't show full speciation, you can observe the beginnings of sub-populations with different trait combinations.
