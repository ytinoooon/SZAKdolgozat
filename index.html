How It Works

Genetic Encoding

Each organism has a chromosome defining its traits including color, size, speed, and more.

Resource Competition

Organisms compete for limited food resources to survive and reproduce.

Reproduction

Successful organisms reproduce, passing traits to offspring with mutations.

Evolution

Over generations, advantageous traits become more common in the population.

Key Concepts

Natural Selection

Organisms with beneficial traits are more likely to survive and reproduce.

Genetic Drift

Random changes in gene frequencies, especially in small populations.

Adaptive Radiation

Diversification of species to fill different ecological niches.
